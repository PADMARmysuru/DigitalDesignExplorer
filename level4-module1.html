1. Moore's Law

In 1965 Gordon Moore observed that the number of transistors on a chip was doubling at a steady pace. Since about 1975 the pace is taken as a doubling roughly every 2 years.

Model: N = 2300 × 2(year − 1971) / 2, starting from the Intel 4004 (about 2,300 transistors, 1971).
Doublings: 0Model transistors: 2,300

Real chips follow this trend only roughly, and it has slowed as transistors approach atomic dimensions.

2. History of IC Invention

1947 – Bardeen, Brattain and Shockley demonstrate the transistor at Bell Labs.
1958 – Jack Kilby (Texas Instruments) builds the first working integrated circuit.
1959 – Robert Noyce (Fairchild) develops the planar silicon IC, which made mass production possible.
1960 – Kahng and Atalla build the first MOSFET at Bell Labs.
1963 – Frank Wanlass invents CMOS logic.
1971 – Intel 4004, the first commercial microprocessor.

ScaleNameTypical transistors per chip*

*Ranges differ between textbooks. Use your prescribed textbook's values in exams.

Two main technology families: bipolar (BJT based, fast but power hungry) and MOS / CMOS (low power, dense, used for almost all modern VLSI).

5. BJT vs MOSFET

FeatureBJTMOSFET
TerminalsBase, Collector, EmitterGate, Drain, Source (+ Body)
ControlCurrent controlled (base current)Voltage controlled (gate voltage)
Input resistanceLow to moderateVery high (insulated gate)
CarriersBoth electrons and holes (bipolar)One type (unipolar)
Static powerHigherVery low in CMOS
Packing densityLowerHigher, easy to scale
Typical useAnalog, high-speed, power stagesDigital VLSI, memory, processors

6. Types of FET

TypeChannelDefault state (VGS = 0)
JFETn or pON (normally conducting)
MOSFET – enhancementn or pOFF, needs gate voltage to form a channel
MOSFET – depletionn or pON, gate voltage can remove the channel
VLSI mostly uses enhancement-mode n-channel and p-channel MOSFETs (NMOS and PMOS).

7. Animated NMOSFET

Move the sliders. Threshold voltage Vt = 1 V.

VGS = 0.0 V
VDS = 0.0 V
Region: CutoffID = 0.00 mA

Rules: VGS < Vt → cutoff. VGS ≥ Vt and VDS < VGS − Vt → linear. Otherwise → saturation (channel pinches off near the drain).

Current values use a simple model with k = 1 mA/V², for teaching only.
