Build a circuit by connecting gate primitives in Verilog.

1. What is Structural Modeling?

Structural modeling describes how gates are connected to form a circuit.

Think of it like drawing a circuit with gates and wires. In Verilog, the gates and their connections are written as code.

Common gate primitives include: and, or, not, nand, nor, xor, xnor.

Build these four stages:

  2. Full Adder 1 uses A[1], B[1], C1 and produces Sum[1], C2.
  3. Full Adder 2 uses A[2], B[2], C2 and produces Sum[2], C3.
  4. Full Adder 3 uses A[3], B[3], C3 and produces Sum[3], Cout.
