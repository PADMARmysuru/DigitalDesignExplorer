🧠 Activity 4 – Behavioral Modeling

Describe how a circuit behaves using always blocks, conditions and case statements.

1. What is Behavioral Modeling?

Behavioral modeling describes what a circuit should do, rather than listing every gate connection.

The main building block is the always block.

Inside it, we can use if, else, case and other decision statements.

💡 Think: “When this event happens, what should the circuit do?”
