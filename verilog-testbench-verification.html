🧪 Activity 6 – Testbench & Verification

Test your Verilog design by applying inputs and checking the outputs.

1. What is a Testbench?

A testbench is a Verilog program used to test a design.

The circuit being tested is called the DUT (Design Under Test).

A simple verification flow is: Apply Inputs → Observe Output → Compare Expected Result

💡 A testbench normally does not have physical input/output ports. It generates stimulus for the DUT.

5. Clock and Reset in a Testbench
