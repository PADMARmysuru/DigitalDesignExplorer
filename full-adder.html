Learn how a Full Adder adds three single-bit binary inputs.

What is a Full Adder?

A Full Adder is a combinational circuit that adds three binary inputs: A, B and Carry-in.

It produces two outputs:

  • SUM
  • CARRY

💻 Verilog Implementation

module full_adder(
    input A,
    input B,
    input Cin,
    output SUM,
    output CARRY
);

assign SUM = A ^ B ^ Cin;

assign CARRY =
       (A & B) |
       (B & Cin) |
       (A & Cin);

endmodule
